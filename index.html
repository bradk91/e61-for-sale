Overview

There are two accidents that I am aware of from before I owned the vehicle. The first one, the previous owner hit a deer sometime around 2020 and had body work done on the front bumper, driver’s fender, hood, and driver’s side headlight. The second one, his wife backed into the side of their garage while attempting to park. This caused some damage to the right rear quarter of the car which has not been repaired - details below.

I purchased the car in 2024 from a gentleman out near Smith Mountain Lake. He had a whole stack of service records and receipts from his ownership but was unwilling to give them to me with the car (I found this somewhat strange, but I believe he had privacy concerns regarding the paperwork). The car had appx. 124,000 miles on it at the time of my purchase.

The car runs and drives well. It never hesitates to start, stumbles, or otherwise indicates that it’s unhappy in any way. My primary reason for selling is that I simply prefer the driving dynamics of the 3-series more than the slightly more comfort-focused 5-series, and would like to acquire a 3-series wagon instead.

Powertrain

Engine

  • The car has the N54 twin turbo engine, which runs quite well. I have never had any of the common N54 issues with this car - trouble starting, rough idle, HPFP issues, etc.
  • The previous owner had the HPFP recall completed by BMW and the car has never given me any fueling issues.
  • I am not certain what index of injectors are installed in the car as I have never felt compelled to take them out and check. They have not given me any issues and I don’t have any reason to believe that any of them are leaking / failing.
  • Neither turbo smokes or otherwise is acting up. The car makes boost and responds as expected.
  • One of the turbos exhibits a very minor amount of wastegate rattle on deceleration only. This is a common N54 issue and at the current level of rattle is specifically regarded as normal operation by BMW.
  • The intake valves were last walnut-blasted by the previous owner at appx. 100k miles.
  • The timing chain and guides appear to be in good condition - there is no cold start rattle, and I have not observed any pieces of guides in the oil.
  • The VANOS solenoids operate as expected, no timing codes or symptoms of failure are present.
  • The Oil Filter Housing gasket is in good condition and does not leak.
  • The cooling system was overhauled by the previous owner at appx. 100k miles, including a new water pump, thermostat, expansion tank, and hoses.
  • The Serpentine Belt and all idlers/tensioners are in good shape, I replaced these in 2024 as preventative maintenance when I purchased the car.
  • The spark plugs and coils are of unknown age, and I have a new set of both (OE Bosch plugs and Denso coils) that I intend to either install before sale or convey to the next owner.
  • The oil has been changed every 5,000 miles under my ownership. I believe the previous owner followed the factory CBS (condition based service) guidelines.
  • There are two minor oil leaks that I am aware of:
  • The valve cover gasket is seeping oil on the passenger side. This oil drips onto the turbo manifold and presents a smell of burnt oil after hard driving. I have a new valve cover gasket (OE Elring) which I intend to install to rectify this issue before sale, otherwise I will convey it to the new owner.
  • The oil pan gasket is leaking slowly at several places around the upper edge where it meets the bottom of the engine block.
  • All lower covers are present and in good condition.

  • The CV axle boots are all in good shape (not torn or leaking) along with the CV axles themselves.
